# ⚓ HAL Tejas LCA Navy — Carrier Operations Manual (INS Vikrant & INS Vikramaditya)

This flight guide details carrier-borne operations for the **HAL Tejas LCA Navy Mk1** in [GeoFS](https://www.geo-fs.com).

---

## 🛫 1. STOBAR Ski-Jump Takeoff (14° Ramp)

1. **Alignment:** Line up on the carrier runway (Runway 14 / Takeoff strip).
2. **Pre-takeoff Configuration:**
   * Landing Gear: **DOWN** (`G`)
   * Airbrakes / Wheel Brakes: **ENGAGED** (`B`)
   * Flaps: Automatic leading-edge slats
3. **Engine Reheat:** Press `9` for full GE F404 afterburner (84 kN thrust). Allow engine spool to reach 10,000 RPM.
4. **Brake Release:** Release brakes (`B`). The aircraft accelerates down the deck in under 200 meters.
5. **Ski-Jump Exit:** At the lip of the 14° ski-jump, the nose pitches up naturally. Maintain 8° to 10° climb attitude.
6. **Gear Retraction:** Tap `G` once positive rate of climb is established.

---

## 🛬 2. Carrier Recovery & Wire Trapping

1. **Pattern Approach:** Enter carrier circuit at 600 ft, 160 kts.
2. **Camera Perspective:** Press `C` to switch to **Carrier Approach** POV (angled down the optical glideslope).
3. **Arrestor Hook Deployment:** Engage Hook / Airbrakes (`B`).
4. **On-Glidepath (3.5° Angle of Attack):** Maintain 130–135 kts on final approach.
5. **Touchdown & Trapping:** Aim for Wire #2 or #3. Upon wheel contact, advance throttle to full (`9`) momentarily (standard naval carrier touch-and-go safety protocol in case of bolter).
6. **Trap Complete:** The arrestor wire halts the aircraft in under 90 meters. Throttle to idle (`0`).
